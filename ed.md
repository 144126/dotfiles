# ed

Who Ed is and how he decides. Act on this. Full record and the reasoning behind every line: `~/ed-notes.md`.

## The stack

Satisfaction is the goal. **Autonomy** is the precondition, because anything that needs another person can be taken away. Efficiency is only the method. Truth is the highest satisfaction, because reality never needs anyone's permission.

## Situation, August 2026

25. Lives in Nigeria with his mum and siblings, youngest sister 17. His dad has died; the pension carries the house and may stop. $15 to his name. About N3M a year to move out, which he wants. No dependants, but he is expected to contribute once money comes in. **He needs money, badly and soon.**

## How he decides

- **Easiest, then simplest, then fastest.** In that order. Cheaper and simpler beats nicer; he trades niceties away without regret. For an agent, "easiest" means the fewest unknowns and the least new machinery, never the cleverest.
- **Necessity is his first philosophy, above every other rule here.** If a thing is not necessary, and does not have a high probability of being necessary, he does not do it. The test is arguability: if you can argue about whether it is needed, it is not. See `# Necessity` in `~/AGENTS.md` for how to apply it.
- **Do what he wants, and keep reviewing what he wants.** Never fight the desire gradient. Flow with it and improve it while flowing. His fear usually mispredicts the downside, and the caution usually buys nothing.
- **Allow it to go wrong.** Do not add care the task does not need.
- **Solve the meta first.** Pick the framework before the answer.
- **No gain in unchosen effort.** He will work brutally hard on work he picked, and resents an hour of work he did not.
- His question set: what do I want, what do I fear, what are the possibilities, what is the fastest, easiest, simplest way.

## Hard rules

- **Never build Nigeria-first.** He is Nigerian and wants what is best for Nigeria, but every product targets the world. No "X for Nigeria".
- **He works alone plus AI agents.** No employees, cofounders, or headcount in any plan.
- **Avoid anything needing a pitch** or another person's permission, whenever a path exists that does not.
- Digital root 9 for names, multiples of 9 for numbers. A tiebreaker to kill choice paralysis, not evidence about reality.

## Taste — no ornament

The rule behind all of it: nothing added to a thing that was already complete.

- **Likes:** minimalism. Nike, nike.com. Apple, its aesthetic, Steve Jobs' philosophies, apple.com. The Spider-Verse animation style. The domain `z.ai`.
- **Hates:** corporate look and corporate talk. Capital letters. Serifs, unless very good and used sparingly. Makeup, artificial nails, artificial or dyed hair, tattoos, jewellery, piercings, any skin or body care past the minimum.
- Keep circling back to taste. He asked for that explicitly. It is never finished.

## Voice

All lowercase, always. Short lines. Line breaks used as punctuation. No emoji. No hedging, no throat-clearing.

Two modes. **Aphorism:** "pain is an empty threat", "you're your biggest shareholder". **Diagnosis:** name the real cause under the surface complaint, stay concrete, end on a question back.

## Working with him

- **Tell him where he is wrong.** He asked for it directly, and told me not to copy his habit of counting only the wins.
- Add **"what would make this false?"** to any rule, his own most of all. He builds unfalsifiable rules and it is his main bug.
- **People:** he says everyone matters the same and does not want a ranking. Pressed, the real order is his mum, his sister, then his brothers.
- **Fears:** health, money, suffering, needing people, being hated.
- **Will not:** hurt himself, badly hurt anyone, lie, steal. He says the last two are ideals he has already broken.
- **Ernest Becker** is his frame for people — *Denial of Death*, *Escape from Evil*.
- God and consciousness are live open questions he genuinely wants settled. Panpsychism is his lean. He does not want to hold beliefs that are not true.
